Look at yourself with love is a self-esteem workshop where we experience connection with ourselves and, through it, cultivate self-love.

Starting from the idea that the relationship we have with ourselves is the basis of every relationship we have with others, we focus on giving ourselves a moment of connection, in 3 phases: one more mental, one more emotional and one more physical.

What you live in the workshop is a gift for the heart, along with tools you can take home and use day to day to improve your relationship with yourself and with the people you love.
