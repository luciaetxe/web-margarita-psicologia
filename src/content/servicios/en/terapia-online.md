Online therapy means talking with your psychologist over video, from home or wherever you are, with the same closeness as in a consulting room. It's the only way I work, and it's what lets me accompany people wherever they live.

## What a session is like

We meet over video for an hour. You choose the place: all it takes is somewhere you can talk without being interrupted. We talk about what you bring that week, what has happened since the last session and what we're working on.

Between sessions you can message me on WhatsApp. If something comes up or you're struggling to hold onto what we discussed, you don't have to wait a week. It's included.

## How often we meet

At first, once a week. Later, every fortnight, depending on how you're doing. There's no fixed number of sessions: we decide together and you can stop whenever you want.

## Who I work with

Adults who demand too much of themselves, live with [anxiety](/en/psicologa-online-ansiedad/), put themselves last or find it hard to set boundaries. I also work with families, children and teenagers, always starting with a call with the parents.

I work in English, Spanish and Catalan. I'm a humanistic psychologist: I listen, I accompany you and I don't judge.

## How to start

Message me on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a free 15-minute call. On that call you tell me what brings you here, I explain how I work and we see whether we're a good fit. If so, we find a slot for the first session. If not, I'll tell you who I think would suit you better.
